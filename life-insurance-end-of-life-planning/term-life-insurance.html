The Basics

Pure, highly affordable life insurance that provides a high coverage amount for a specific, designated timeframe (such as 10, 20, or 30 years) during your peak earning and family-rearing years.

Term Life Insurance

The Details

Term life insurance provides pure, high-value death benefit protection for a specific, designated timeframe — such as 10, 20, or 30 years. Because it does not incorporate complex cash-value investment components, term insurance delivers maximum financial protection during your peak working, earning, and child-rearing years at the most economical monthly premium rates available. If the insured individual passes away during the active term, the policy pays out the full tax-free death benefit to beneficiaries. If the term expires, coverage concludes or can be reviewed based on updated life circumstances.
