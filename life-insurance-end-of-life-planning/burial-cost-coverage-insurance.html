Burial Cost / End of Life Planning

The Details

Dedicated burial cost planning utilizes specialized, small-face-value whole life insurance policies engineered explicitly to handle final service obligations. These plans are specifically tailored to cover funeral home contracts, professional services, caskets or urns, cemetery plots, grave markers, and memorial service gatherings. By putting a dedicated burial fund in place, you establish a dedicated financial resource that bypasses complicated probate delays and ensures immediate cash availability for your family.

Our Approach

How WRA Puts Together Your Plan

We help you document your exact personal preferences for your final arrangements and match you with small-face-value policies explicitly structured for fast benefit release. This ensures your precise final wishes are fully pre-funded without requiring family members to dip into personal savings accounts.
