The Basics

Financial contracts designed to replace your traditional workplace paycheck in retirement, providing a predictable, steady stream of income that is completely immune to stock market volatility.

Guaranteed Lifetime Income / Annuities

The Details

Guaranteed lifetime income vehicles, primarily structured through fixed and indexed annuities, are financial contracts issued by insurance companies designed to replace your traditional workplace paycheck during retirement. When you transition out of the workforce, you lose steady corporate-style cash flow, shifting your primary financial challenge from wealth accumulation to secure asset distribution. Annuities establish a dependable financial safety floor, providing predictable income payments for a set period or guaranteed for the rest of your life, regardless of how severe stock market downturns or economic recessions become.

Our Approach

How WRA Puts Together Your Plan

We calculate your essential monthly retirement expenses (housing, utilities, food, and healthcare) to establish a reliable baseline income floor. We then analyze fixed and indexed annuity structures to construct a steady, predictable retirement paycheck tailored to your exact lifestyle needs while insulating your core savings from Wall Street volatility.
