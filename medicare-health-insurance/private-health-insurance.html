Private Health Insurance (Under 65)

The Details

Private individual health insurance plans provide legally compliant medical coverage for individuals who have not yet reached the federal Medicare eligibility age of 65. This category covers a broad demographic, including self-employed entrepreneurs, small business owners, early retirees leaving corporate careers before 65, and spouses who require independent coverage. These plans are structured around varying metal tiers (such as Bronze, Silver, or Gold) that dictate how medical costs are split between your monthly premium payments and your out-of-pocket deductibles and copays when visiting doctors or filling prescriptions.
