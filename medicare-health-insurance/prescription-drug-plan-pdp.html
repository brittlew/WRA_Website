Prescription Drug Plan (PDP)

Standalone prescription drug plans protect your budget from escalating pharmaceutical tier costs by matching your precise medication regimen to the most cost-effective formulary.

Who it's for: Anyone on Medicare taking routine medications who wants to minimize pharmacy costs.

Why it matters: Drug formularies change yearly, and the wrong plan can lead to high out-of-pocket tier costs for essential prescriptions.

The Basics

The Basics

Standalone prescription drug plans protect your budget from rising pharmaceutical costs by covering routine pharmacy prescriptions that Original Medicare leaves out.

The Details

The Details

Standalone Prescription Drug Plans (often referred to as Medicare Part D) are federally approved private insurance policies designed to help beneficiaries manage the cost of outpatient prescription medications. Original Medicare generally does not pay for routine prescriptions filled at retail pharmacies. Part D plans utilize specific drug "formularies" — lists of covered medications categorized into distinct pricing tiers ranging from generic drugs to specialized brand-name treatments. Each plan has its own unique formulary, rules, and pharmacy network, meaning the cost of the exact same medication can vary significantly from one insurance provider to another.

It is important to keep a current list of your medications so that we can find you the best plan with the most coverage.

How WRA Puts Together Your Plan
