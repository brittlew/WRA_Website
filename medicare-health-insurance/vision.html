The Basics

Standalone or bundled vision care plans that cover routine eye examinations, eyeglasses, frames, and contact lenses outside of standard medical insurance.

Vision

The Details

Standalone or bundled vision insurance plans provide dedicated financial coverage for routine eye care services that are typically excluded from standard medical policies and Original Medicare. This includes annual preventive eye examinations, refraction tests, and allowances toward corrective prescription eyewear such as frames, standard lenses, progressive coatings, or contact lenses. Maintaining routine vision coverage is essential not only for visual acuity and everyday comfort, but also for the early detection of age-related systemic health conditions like glaucoma, cataracts, and diabetic retinopathy during comprehensive eye exams.
