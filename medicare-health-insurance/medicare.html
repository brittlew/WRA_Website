How It Works

How It Works

Original Medicare is the federal government's healthcare program for people turning 65 or qualifying due to specific disabilities. It is divided into two primary parts that form your foundational medical safety net. Part A functions as your hospital insurance, helping cover inpatient hospital stays, skilled nursing facility care, hospice, and certain home health services. Most individuals receive Part A premium-free if they or their spouse paid Medicare payroll taxes for at least 10 years. Part B acts as your medical insurance, covering doctor visits, outpatient care, durable medical equipment, and preventive clinical screenings. Unlike Part A, Part B requires a standard monthly premium that can scale based on your individual income level. Together, Original Medicare covers roughly 80% of approved medical expenses, leaving the remaining 20% coinsurance uncovered without an annual out-of-pocket spending cap.
