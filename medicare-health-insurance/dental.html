Dental

The Basics

Standalone or bundled dental plans that help cover routine cleanings, fillings, and major restorative work like crowns or bridges outside of standard Medicare coverage.

Dental

The Details

Independent dental insurance plans fill a major coverage void left by standard Medicare, which explicitly excludes routine dental cleanings, fillings, tooth extractions, root canals, crowns, bridges, and dentures. Dental policies typically operate on a structured coinsurance framework (often referred to as a "100/80/50" structure), providing full coverage for preventative checkups, moderate coverage for basic restorative work like fillings, and shared cost-sharing for major restorative procedures. Securing independent dental coverage protects fixed retirement budgets from sudden, expensive out-of-pocket dental bills.
