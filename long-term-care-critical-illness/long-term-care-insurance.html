Long Term Care Insurance

Long Term Care Insurance

Long-term care protection secures funds for nursing, assisted living, or in-home care assistance, ensuring physical care needs do not exhaust your lifetime savings.

Who it's for: Adults in their 50s and 60s who want to ensure they receive quality care in old age without burdening their children or depleting their estate.

Why it matters: Extended care (assisted living, in-home nursing, or nursing home care) can cost thousands monthly, quickly wiping out a lifetime of retirement savings.

Planning timeline: Best purchased in your 50s or early 60s while premiums are manageable and you maintain good health.

Partner advantage: We structure modern hybrid life/long-term care solutions that protect your assets while guaranteeing care funds if needed.

Long Term Care Insurance

The Basics

Insurance coverage specifically designed to pay for extended support services — such as in-home nursing assistance, assisted living, or nursing home care — when you can no longer perform daily living activities independently.

Long Term Care Insurance

The Details

Long-term care insurance provides dedicated financial support for individuals who require extended personal assistance with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) — such as bathing, dressing, eating, transferring, and continence care — due to chronic illness, cognitive impairment, or physical frailty. Standard health insurance and traditional Medicare do not pay for custodial long-term care in nursing homes, assisted living facilities, or in-home professional nursing care. Without dedicated coverage, extended custodial care can cost thousands of dollars per month, quickly depleting a lifetime of accumulated retirement savings and liquidating family estates.
